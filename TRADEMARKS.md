# ARA name and visual identity

## Current status

“ARA”, “ARA Platform” / “Plataforma ARA”, “Learning Resources Environment” / “Ambiente de Recursos de Aprendizagem”, the kanji, logo and visual identity identify the official project. They are **not** granted under the software or documentation licenses.

The project does not currently claim that these signs are registered trademarks. Do not use the `®` symbol. Formal trademark searches, ownership analysis and possible registration in Brazil and Europe remain pending.

## Uses that are normally acceptable

Subject to applicable law and accurate attribution, the following descriptive uses are normally acceptable:

- referring to the ARA project in research, teaching, journalism or documentation;
- stating that a system is based on, derived from or compatible with ARA;
- linking to the official repository;
- reproducing the unmodified official software while accurately identifying its source;
- discussing, reviewing or comparing the platform.

These uses must not imply sponsorship, certification, endorsement or official status when none exists.

## Uses that require prior permission

Prior written permission is required before:

- presenting a modified fork as the official ARA Platform;
- using ARA, the kanji or the official logo as the main identity of a derived product or commercial service;
- using names such as “ARA Enterprise”, “ARA Official”, “ARA Premium” or similar designations that may imply an official edition;
- registering domains, organizations, applications or services whose naming may create confusion with the official project;
- claiming certification, approval, institutional partnership or endorsement by the ARA project.

## Forks and white-label deployments

The software license permits use, modification and commercial services under its terms. Modified versions should adopt their own name and visual identity. They may truthfully state that they are based on ARA.

Institutional white-label deployments may replace the displayed name, logo, colours and domain. Use of the official ARA identity in a modified or institutionally distributed edition requires a separate authorization or a future published trademark policy.

## No effect on software freedoms

This policy concerns project identity and indication of origin. It does not reduce the permissions granted by the software and documentation licenses.
